## Overview

I study the demographics of recovering whale and dolphin populations: how fast animals grow, what condition they are in, how they group together, and how a changing ocean shapes all of it. This work combines aerial photogrammetry, long-term monitoring, and environmental data to track population health and the pressures acting on it.
