## Overview

Whale and dolphin tourism is the most common way people encounter cetaceans, and the sustainability of the industry depends on the animals remaining healthy and abundant. My research examines how vessels and swimmers influence cetacean behaviour and when those effects are biologically meaningful, generating the evidence needed for effective management. This has grown into a body of work spanning multiple species, regions, and tourism models, developed in collaboration with the operators and regulators responsible for managing these interactions.
