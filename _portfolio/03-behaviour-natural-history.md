## Overview

Understanding how to protect a species begins with understanding how it lives. Despite decades of research, much of the behaviour and ecology of whales and dolphins remains poorly described. My research documents these behaviours through long-term field studies, revealing how animals move, socialise, reproduce, and use important habitats. By building this foundation of natural history, the work provides context for ecological research and informs conservation and management.
