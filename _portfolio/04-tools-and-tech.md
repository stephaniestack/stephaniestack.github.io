## Overview

I apply emerging tools and technologies in new ways to study whales and dolphins from a distance, with as little disturbance as possible. Rather than building the technology myself, I adapt it to answer biological questions that are otherwise hard to reach: using aerial photogrammetry to measure body size and condition, comparing imaging platforms, and applying epigenetic methods to estimate the age of individual whales. I am extending this toolkit toward animal-borne tags and biologging, with papers from the tagging work forthcoming and appearing here as they are published.
