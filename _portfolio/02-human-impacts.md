## Overview

Whales and dolphins share their habitat with fishing gear, vessels, and coastal development, yet many harmful interactions are never observed or reported. My research develops methods to quantify these hidden impacts by combining drone photogrammetry and long-term photo-identification to measure injury rates, estimate the prevalence of entanglement and vessel collisions, and identify the human activities placing populations at greatest risk. These data provide an evidence base for decisions about shipping, fisheries, and habitat protection.
